03 · Websites & Systems

Scoped and quoted, not shelf-priced.

Why it's quote-based

A landing page and a billing-integrated client portal cost very different amounts to build properly. A fixed price list would either overcharge simple work or underquote complex work — so every build gets scoped first, free of charge.

What affects the number

Page count, custom integrations (payments, Mobile Money, third-party APIs), and whether it needs a client-facing dashboard are the main drivers of scope and cost.

What you always get

A written scope and price before any code is touched, a staging link during the build, and full source code and documentation at handover — regardless of project size.
