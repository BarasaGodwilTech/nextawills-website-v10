Why we exist

We believe technology should keep helping after launch.

Started from a real gap

We kept meeting businesses that had been sold a plan, then left to figure out backups, renewals, and outages entirely on their own. Nextawills exists to be the team that doesn't disappear after the invoice.

Infrastructure and software, together

Splitting "who built it" from "who hosts it" is where most support tickets go to die. We do both, so one team can actually see the whole picture when something needs fixing.

Built for how Uganda actually pays

Mobile Money billing, local support hours, and plain-language invoices — not a platform designed elsewhere and localized as an afterthought.

What we hold ourselves to

Three things we care about.

01

Be clear from the start

If a plan won't fit your traffic, we say so before you pay — not after you've outgrown it in six weeks.

02

Keep things understandable

You get real access and real documentation. Nothing is withheld to make switching providers harder than it should be.

03

Be there when it matters

A monitored alert gets a person, not a queue position. That's the whole support model — everything else is detail.

How we got here

Small enough to stay close to the work.

Early on

A handful of sites, hosted properly

The first accounts were small businesses that just needed something to work reliably — no reseller book, no automation, just careful setup and someone to call.

Growing footprint

Custom software joins the stack

As clients asked for dashboards and internal tools on top of their hosting, the Websites & Systems side of the team formed to build directly against the infrastructure we already managed.

Today

A broader technology practice

Hosting, software, domains, and support now run as a single accountable team — sized to a single site or a full reseller book of clients, without losing the "someone will actually answer" feel we started with.
